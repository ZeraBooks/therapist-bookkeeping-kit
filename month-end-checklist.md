# Month-end checklist: therapy practice

For counselors, psychologists, marriage and family therapists and social
workers in private practice, solo or group. Work top to bottom. No session
notes, diagnoses or client records are needed for any step.

Month: ____________  Closed by: ____________  Date locked: ____________

## 1. Get everything in
- [ ] Bank and card statements for the full month, as PDFs
- [ ] Card processor monthly statement
- [ ] Payments report from your practice software, by clinician and payer type
- [ ] Remittance advice for every insurance EFT received in the month
- [ ] Clinician split report, if you pay contract clinicians a share

## 2. Insurance EFTs
- [ ] Each EFT in the bank matched to its remittance advice
- [ ] Booked as insurance revenue, by payer if you track panels
- [ ] Any EFT with no remittance, or a remittance with no EFT, listed to
      follow up

## 3. Self-pay and card fees
- [ ] Card deposits grossed back up to what clients paid
- [ ] Processor fees booked as merchant fees
- [ ] Late-cancellation and no-show fees in their own account
- [ ] Superbill clients booked as self-pay; nothing extra is owed to the
      practice when their insurer reimburses them

## 4. Clinician splits
- [ ] Collections by clinician add up to total collections for the month
- [ ] Each contract clinician paid their agreed rate on what they collected,
      not on sessions booked
- [ ] Any split worked out but not yet paid held as contract clinician pay
      owed

## 5. Practice costs
- [ ] Software, telehealth, liability insurance, licensing, CE and
      supervision booked to their own accounts
- [ ] Annual premiums or renewals paid this month spread over the months they
      cover, if you keep accrual books

## 6. Owner pay
- [ ] Owner draws or salary kept out of expenses

## 7. Reconcile and review
- [ ] Every bank and card account ties to its printed closing balance
- [ ] Revenue by payer compared with last month
- [ ] Uncategorized at zero

## 8. Lock and send
- [ ] Month locked so a late edit can't change it
- [ ] Reports sent to the owner, with open questions listed
